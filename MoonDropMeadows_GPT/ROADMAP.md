# Development Roadmap

## 0.1 - First playable town
- [x] MonoGame project and built-in pixel font.
- [x] Character name, starting age, and family setup.
- [x] Walkable town, nearby interactions, HUD, and save data.
- [x] Town NPC conversations and relationship values.
- [x] First farming loop and basic shops.
- [x] School attendance and career selection.
- [x] Town computer, newspaper, business start, land purchase, and hiring hooks.
- [x] Friendship, dating, partnership, marriage, and family data foundation.
- [x] Bob and MAYORLARRY's first Moonwell quest.
- [ ] Original character sprites, portraits, tiles, and animations.
- [ ] Run Windows CI build and fix platform-specific issues.

## 0.2 - A bigger week
NPC schedules and calendars; school classes, clubs, grades, exams, and childhood events; shop inventories, clothing previews, furniture, home decoration; dance/festival rhythm mini-game; more crops, fishing, foraging, cooking, and crafting; job shifts and skill progression.

## 0.3 - People remember
Relationship memories, birthdays, dates, anniversaries, gifts, friendship stories; wedding events, household management, parenting choices, child growth stages; family tree, scrapbook, portraits, generational visits; more residents and moving-in events.

## 0.4 - Build a business
Detailed business dashboard and product supply chains; employee skills, wages, morale, roles, and schedules; local recruitment and relocating residents; land lots, construction, store upgrades, interior design; company reputation and town economy.

## 0.5 - Story expansion
Full Bob questline and wizard tower; MAYORLARRY's council storyline; newspaper mystery and old town archive; Moonwood, caves, Moonwell, multi-year story; branching choices and town futures.

## 0.6 - Audio, art, and accessibility
In-game optional voice recorder and WAV take manager; original character portraits, tile sets, clothing, and UI art; music, ambient sound, dance tracks, and sliders; controller-first menus, touch controls, remapping, accessibility options.

## 1.0 - A town worth staying in
Full seasonal calendar and events; complete first-generation story and family progression; multiple careers and fully playable business paths; more buildings, residents, land, and customization; cross-platform packaging and release testing.

New systems should join the same save model and world simulation rather than being bolted on as unrelated mini-games.
